## What “Accredited” Means for Online Physics Classes

When students search for **accredited online physics classes**, they usually mean one of two things:

1. The course is offered by a **regionally accredited college or university** and appears on an official transcript as regular credit.
2. The course is from an **ACE-evaluated provider**, and their college is willing to accept ACE CREDIT recommendations.{' '}
   <a class="link-external" href="https://www.straighterline.com/blog/straighterline-launches-2-new-online-college-physics-courses-expands-curriculum-to-38-courses" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">StraighterLine: online college physics</a>

For example, Oregon State University’s Ecampus runs a full online physics sequence that includes algebra-based and calculus-based options. These are regular OSU courses that fulfill OSU’s science-with-lab core requirement, with credits on a traditional university transcript. Michigan State University describes its online and virtual physics offerings the same way—online delivery, but the same course numbers and degree applicability as on-campus sections.{' '}
<a class="link-external" href="https://pa.msu.edu/academics/online-courses.aspx" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">MSU online courses</a>.

On the alternative-credit side, StraighterLine’s General Physics I has been evaluated by ACE. ACE’s National Guide lists it as lower-division baccalaureate credit, which lets partner colleges—and some non-partners—apply it toward a degree when they honor the recommendation.{' '}
<a class="link-external" href="https://www.straighterline.com/online-college-courses/general-physics-i/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">StraighterLine General Physics I</a>.

## How Transferable Online Physics Credit Works

Even when a course is offered by a reputable university or appears in ACE’s guide, your **home institution** still decides whether it counts the way you need. The registrar or department will look at questions like:

- Is this algebra-based or calculus-based physics?
- Does it include a lab, or is it lecture-only?
- Does the level match our own Physics I/II sequence?

Southern California University of Health Sciences (SCU) makes this explicit. Their accelerated Physics I and II courses are regionally accredited and aimed at students completing prerequisites for nursing, physical therapy, pharmacy, and other health programs. You can enroll in lecture only, lab only, or a combined lecture-and-lab package in several online and in-person formats—but SCU still tells students to confirm transfer with their institution before paying.{' '}
<a class="link-external" href="https://acceleratedsciences.scuhs.edu/course-listing/physics-i/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">SCU Accelerated Sciences: Physics I</a>.

If you are stacking science prerequisites, see our{' '}
<a href="/resources/blog/prerequisite-courses-for-nursing-school">nursing prerequisites guide</a> and{' '}
<a href="/resources/blog/prerequisite-college-courses-by-major">prerequisite courses by major</a> overview.

Blue Ridge Community College’s online General College Physics II (PHY 202) illustrates statewide transfer tagging: the course is listed as a **Passport and UCGS transfer course** in Virginia’s system, meaning it is pre-mapped into guaranteed transfer blocks for many in-state public universities. Colorado’s statewide online consortium offers a fully online calculus-based Physics I with lab (PHY 2111) with GT Pathways designation in the physical/life sciences category.{' '}
<a class="link-external" href="https://courses.vccs.edu/colleges/brcc/courses/PHY202-GeneralCollegePhysicsII" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Blue Ridge PHY 202</a>.

The bottom line: **accredited + online is not enough**. You need the right level and lab status, and you need your future school to sign off.

## Main Types of Accredited Online Physics Providers

### 1. University-run online physics with lab

Traditional universities offering their own physics sequence online:

- **Oregon State University Ecampus** — Algebra-based and calculus-based physics fully online, including sequences such as PH 211–213 (calculus-based). Labs use hands-on activities with common materials, guided work, and remote support.{' '}
  <a class="link-external" href="https://www.vernier.com/vernier-ideas/ecampus-how-oregon-state-brings-hands-on-learning-to-online-physics-courses/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Vernier: OSU online physics labs</a>
- **Michigan State University** — Virtual sections award the same credit and satisfy the same degree requirements as on-campus classes for qualified students.{' '}
  <a class="link-external" href="https://www.mtu.edu/physics/online/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Michigan Tech / MSU online physics</a>
- **University of New England (UNE) PHYS 1010** — Algebra-based Physics I with lecture and lab combined into a four-credit, fully online course covering kinematics, forces, energy, momentum, and fluids.{' '}
  <a class="link-external" href="https://online.une.edu/science-prerequisites/online-physics-i/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">UNE Online Physics I</a>

These options appeal when you want a familiar university name and a clear “Physics I with Lab” title on the transcript.

### 2. Community colleges and statewide online consortia

Community colleges are strong options for in-state transfer:

- **Colorado Community College System – Colorado Online (PHY 2111)** — Calculus-based Physics I/Lab online with a shipped lab kit and GT Pathways status for Colorado public universities.{' '}
  <a class="link-external" href="https://erpdnssb.cccs.edu/PRODLCC/bwckctlg.p_disp_listcrse?term_in=202630&subj_in=PHY&crse_in=2111&schd_in=LEL" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Colorado Online PHY 2111</a>
- **Blue Ridge Community College – PHY 202** — Distance learning with integrated lab hours and Passport/UCGS transfer designations in Virginia.{' '}
  <a class="link-external" href="https://courses.vccs.edu/colleges/brcc/courses/PHY202-GeneralCollegePhysicsII" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Blue Ridge PHY 202</a>
- **EDUKAN** — Consortium of Kansas community colleges offering online PHS251/PHS252 (5-credit physics) with credit transcribed through a member college.{' '}
  <a class="link-external" href="https://edukan.edu/course_category/physics/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">EDUKAN physics courses</a>

### 3. Accelerated and post-bacc physics for health programs

Providers focused on career-changers and prerequisite timelines:

- **SCU Accelerated Sciences** — Short, intense Physics I and II with lecture-only, lab-only, or combined enrollment (online self-paced, live online, or in-person).{' '}
  <a class="link-external" href="https://acceleratedsciences.scuhs.edu/course-listing/physics-i/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">SCU Physics I</a>
- **UNE science prerequisites** — Self-paced physics and related sciences for health-program applicants.{' '}
  <a class="link-external" href="https://online.une.edu/science-prerequisites/online-physics-i/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">UNE science prerequisites</a>

## How Online Physics Labs Actually Work

Physics labs are where online formats get tricky—many programs insist on **real lab work**, not simulations alone. Common approaches:

- **Hands-on labs with everyday materials** — OSU Ecampus labs use household or easily purchased materials plus written reports and discussions, while still fulfilling science-with-lab core requirements.{' '}
  <a class="link-external" href="https://physics.oregonstate.edu/undergraduate/ecampus" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">OSU Ecampus physics</a>
- **Lab kits shipped home** — Colorado’s PHY 2111 uses a physical kit with documented timelines and proctoring guidance.{' '}
  <a class="link-external" href="https://erpdnssb.cccs.edu/PRODLCC/bwckctlg.p_disp_listcrse?term_in=202630&subj_in=PHY&crse_in=2111&schd_in=LEL" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Colorado PHY 2111 catalog</a>
- **Separate lecture and lab enrollment** — SCU lets you take lecture only, lab only, or combined bundles—useful when a graduate program requires a distinct lab line on the transcript.{' '}
  <a class="link-external" href="https://acceleratedsciences.scuhs.edu/course-listing/physics-ii/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">SCU Physics II</a>
- **Integrated community-college labs** — Blue Ridge’s PHY 202 codes lab hours into a four-credit distance course for transfer.{' '}
  <a class="link-external" href="https://courses.vccs.edu/colleges/brcc/courses/PHY202-GeneralCollegePhysicsII" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Blue Ridge PHY 202</a>

## How to Choose the Right Accredited Online Physics Course

If you are choosing a course today, use this path:

- Start with **regionally accredited colleges** (university or community college) that already serve online and visiting students.
- Match your **math level and major** (pre-med/nursing often = algebra-based; engineering/physics major often = calculus-based).{' '}
  <a class="link-external" href="https://pa.msu.edu/academics/online-courses.aspx" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">MSU online course listings</a>
- Make sure the course includes a **lab** if you need a lab science.{' '}
  <a class="link-external" href="https://online.une.edu/science-prerequisites/online-physics-i/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">UNE Physics I with lab</a>
- Choose a format that fits your schedule—term-based, self-paced, or accelerated—and confirm **proctored exams** and **lab kit** logistics before the course starts.{' '}
  <a class="link-external" href="https://ecampus.oregonstate.edu/soc/ecatalog/ecoursedetail.htm?subject=PH&coursenumber=212&termcode=ALL" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">OSU Ecampus course detail</a>

When you do those checks up front, accredited **physics college courses online** can clear one of the toughest science requirements on your degree plan without giving up flexibility or transferability. Explore{' '}

<a href="/resources/blog/">blog</a> for more transfer-credit guides.
